# F0.1
A little aerobic toy car born to be out of wasted can obliged to be 3D printed. It can be controlled by your phone using dabble!

Before getting into the technical details ı would like to share my story of building this project. One night I was sitting on my desk just overthinking and doing nothing as usual. Saw my deodorant can, it was empty and thought why not build something with it and the idea of building a decorative F1 car came to my mind. I just ran into our schools workshop and started cutting some parts from paper. I just sticked them to the deodorant can's surface which I straightened before dutting the papers.
